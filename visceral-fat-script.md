# The Fat You Can't Pinch — Script

Format: vertical 9:16 · about 1:30 · read by NARRATOR ROB · about 205 words

Voice: NARRATOR ROB, calm and warm, unhurried. Short pause at every "…".

## 1. Narration (word for word)

1. Your most dangerous fat is the one you can't pinch… and crunches won't touch it.
2. Under your belly skin sits the soft fat you can grab.
3. But deeper, behind your stomach muscles, another fat wraps around your organs.
4. It's called visceral fat.
5. And it's not just storage.
6. It releases fatty acids and inflammatory signals, and its blood drains straight to your liver.
7. Too much is linked to type 2 diabetes and heart disease.
8. Here's the good news: it usually shrinks faster than the fat you can pinch.
9. You can't spot-burn it, so a thousand sit-ups won't target it.
10. But steady cardio, like brisk walking, cycling or swimming, shrinks it… even when the scale barely moves.
11. Cut sugary drinks. Your liver turns their extra sugar into fat.
12. Go easy on alcohol.
13. Eat more fiber, from beans, oats and vegetables.
14. Sleep seven to nine hours. Short sleep is tied to more belly fat.
15. And lift something heavy, so you keep muscle while the fat comes off.
16. Want a quick check? Grab a piece of string.
17. Cut it to your height, fold it in half, and wrap it around your waist at your belly button.
18. If the ends don't meet, it's time to start.
19. Small, steady changes. Your organs will feel it.
20. Follow for more facts at your fingertips.
